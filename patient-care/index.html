Patient Care

Everything you need to know before, during and after your visit — so you can focus on getting better.

Admission

Admission & discharge

Our admissions team walks you through every step and keeps your family informed.

1

Registration

Present your NRIC/passport, insurance card and doctor’s referral at the admission counter.

2

Eligibility & deposit

We verify insurance/panel eligibility and advise on any deposit required for your room class.

3

Room assignment

You’ll be escorted to your room and introduced to your nurse and care team.

4

Discharge

Your doctor explains your treatment, medication and follow-up. Final bill is settled at discharge.

Our Commitment

Your rights & responsibilities

We believe that informed patients make confident decisions. These commitments guide every conversation and every interaction at Sunway Medical Center.

  • To receive safe, respectful and dignified care regardless of background.
  • To be given clear information about your diagnosis, treatment and costs.
  • To privacy and confidentiality of your medical records under the PDPA.
  • To seek a second opinion and to decline any treatment.
  • To voice concerns, compliments or complaints without prejudice.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an appointment to see a specialist?

An appointment is recommended as it reduces waiting time. Walk-in patients are accepted subject to doctor availability. Book online via our Make Appointment page.

What should I bring for my first visit?

Your NRIC or passport, insurance card or guarantee letter, referral letter (if any), and a list of your current medications.

Can I choose my own doctor?

Yes. You may select a preferred specialist when booking. If you have no preference, we’ll assign the next available doctor.

How do I get my test results?

Most results are ready the same day. Your doctor will review them with you, or our team will contact you to arrange a follow-up.

What are your payment options?

We accept cash, major credit/debit cards and selected e-wallets. For insured patients, please present your GL before admission.

Have a question about your care?

Our patient care team is happy to help — reach us by text or email.
